# Initial Rule Book

- CUS-001: Customers do not leave because they wait.
- CUS-002: Customers leave only after buy, sale, or rejection.
- LC-001: Boss first-clear takes priority while available.
- LC-002: Repeatable Lost City quests may be farmed.
- LC-003: FULL mode may use all available parties.
- LC-004: FULL mode must be manually configurable.
- COL-001: Party reservation is configurable.
- MKT-001: Automation must never spend Gems.
